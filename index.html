Installation (Android)

Sideload the APK in four steps — illustrated below. After that, set up your account (steps 5–7).

First launch

  1. Create a cloud account under Account → Create account (Free tier).
  2. Send a short email with your username to support@gridiron-iq.de — we manually enable Pro for beta testers.
  3. Connect your Sleeper account under Settings and enable your leagues.

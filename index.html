Installation (Android)

  1. Download app-release.apk from the release above.
  2. Allow installs from unknown sources for your browser or file manager (Settings → Security / Apps).
  3. Open the APK and confirm installation.
  4. Launch GridIron IQ and create a cloud account under Account → Create account (Free tier).
  5. Send a short email with your username to support@gridiron-iq.de — we manually enable Pro for beta testers.
  6. Connect your Sleeper account under Settings and enable your leagues.
